## 单镜头 MJ 变体规则

- 后备箱俯拍：在 `S01` 中改成 `45mm close overhead insert shot of the open trunk, cluttered family items, old basketball rolling out`。
- 车库对峙：在 `S01` 中加入 `two Chinese men facing each other beside the white EV and red convertible, basketball on wet concrete floor, 50mm medium confrontation shot`。
- 敞篷车内：沿用 `S01` 冷绿车库影调，场景改为 `inside the red convertible cabin, leather seat, awkward intimate evidence, low angle 35mm shot`。
- MPV 后排：沿用 `S03` 冷绿日间影调，场景改为 `inside luxury MPV rear seat, screen glow, window daylight, secretary holding tablet`。
- 狗视角：沿用 `S04` 暖中性自然光，构图改为 `24mm dog-eye-level tracking perspective through rural alley`。
- 火车/黑车/送水工单镜头：沿用 `S05` 冷青运动影调，只保留一个真实空间，不要拼贴。
